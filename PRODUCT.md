# Product Truth: maniratansingh.com

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
Web (Next.js 16 App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Three.js)

## Subject & Identity
Mani Ratan Singh (MRPS) — Technologist, Designer, and Maker.
A personal luxury digital space at `maniratansingh.com`, connecting software systems, hardware builds, and tactile interface themes.

## Target Audience
Design directors, founders, creative engineers, and technologists who appreciate high-craft digital materiality, embedded hardware, and optical UI design.

## Purpose & Mode
- **Mode:** `Experience` (Portfolio & Creative Showcase).
- **Core Goal:** The website should feel like a beautifully crafted digital space belonging to a human creator—not a SaaS company, agency, or generic developer résumé. The artifact leads from the first viewport; the interface recedes.

## Brand Commitments
- **Personality:** Tactile, restrained, personal, luxury, curious.
- **Anti-References ("AI Slop" to ban):**
  - No kicker / eyebrow labels above headings (`INDEX [01/05]`, `01 / BIOGRAPHY`).
  - No identical-size card grids (cards as lazy containers).
  - No monospace as a costume for "technical".
  - No gradient text; emphasis comes from typographic scale and weight.
  - No flat gray boxes pretending to be glass; glass must carry physical optical depth, caustics, and edge lighting.
  - No repeated identical entrance fades on every paragraph.

## Primary Anchor
- **Primary Hub:** `mrps.in`
- **Key Artifacts:** Jellyfin Liquid Glass, mrps.in, Aesthetic Vinyl Player, RP2040 OLED Macropad, WiFi Clock, Legal AI Batch Processor.
